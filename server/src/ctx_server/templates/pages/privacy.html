Privacy

What we store. Your email address and name from the sign-in provider you choose (Google or GitHub), and the repositories you create: the task-state items you or your AI assistant commit, their history, and an audit log of how each commit was processed. Uploaded chat exports are kept for at most 30 days.

Who processes it. To detect duplicates and contradictions, item text is sent to Google Cloud Vertex AI (embedding and language models). Repository owners can turn this off per repository. Data is stored on Google Cloud in the region this service runs in.

Who can see it. Only people you share a repository with, in the role you give them. Stored content is never sold or used for advertising.

Deleting it. Deleting a repository permanently removes its items, history, audit log and uploads. To delete your account, contact the operator of this service.
